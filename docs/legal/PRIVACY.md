**Status:** Placeholder for public beta-open. Not legal advice.

HummingBird Platform (“HBP”) respects your privacy. This stub summarizes intent; a counsel-reviewed policy will ship before beta-open.

## Data we collect

- **Account:** email, name, authentication identifiers.
- **Usage:** API requests, collaboration presence, workflow runs (aggregated metrics).
- **Content:** hardware artifacts you upload (HNF, KiCad, etc.) stored in your project namespaces.

## How we use data

- Operate HOS, collaboration, and workflow services.
- Security, abuse prevention, and support.
- Product improvement (aggregated, non-content analytics).

## Sharing

- We do not sell personal data.
- Subprocessors (e.g. cloud host, Stripe for billing) process data under contract.
- We may disclose if required by law.

## Retention

- Account data while active + reasonable backup retention (see restore drill RPO).
- You may request deletion subject to legal holds.
